Who we are
The platform

A Saudi platform built on verified offers

Deal Pulse KSA is a wholly Saudi-owned platform that brings brands' offers and buying content together in one place, and delivers them to the shopper at the exact moment they are searching. We run on one rule we do not bend: no offer is published before it is verified — because an expired code damages the brand's reputation before it damages ours.

Vision

The most trusted reference for a real deal

To be the most trusted reference for discovering offers in the Kingdom and the region — not through the longest list, but the most accurate and honest content.

Mission

A smarter purchase in less time

To help the Saudi shopper make an informed decision that saves their time and money — and to help the brand reach them at the moment of intent, not before it.

Where orders leak
Where orders are lost

The promo code field takes your buyer
out of your store. Do they come back?

This happens in your store today, with or without us: the buyer reaches checkout, sees an empty "promo code" field, and leaves to find one. That minute decides between a completed order and an abandoned cart.

Without us the loop breaks at step 2: they search, do not find your code, and either buy with a competitor's or abandon the cart. We do not create this moment; we make it far likelier that it ends with you.
Three questions usually asked here

How is the order attributed to us?

Through your own affiliate system, or the network you subscribe to: an exclusive code or link in our name, with every order recorded on your side.

And if nobody buys?

No discount is given except on an order that completed. A visitor who did not buy costs you nothing — cost is tied to result, not exposure.

Would you send our buyer to a competitor?

No. Your brand page with us carries no competing offer and no diversion to one, even where the competing discount is larger. That is simply how we work.

Trust & principles
How we treat your brand

Your brand in hands that will not trade on it

Working with a third party that publishes under your brand's name is a risk before it is an opportunity. These are the principles we work by, put in front of you early so you know how we think — the terms of any collaboration are settled in a separate agreement.

No unverified offer published

Every offer is tested before it goes live and pulled the moment it expires. We do not leave a dead code carrying your name.

No bidding on your brand name

We do not bid on your brand name or its variants in paid search. We do not compete for your own visitor, and we do not inflate your keyword cost.

No competitor code beside yours

Your brand page carries no competing offer and no diversion to one — even where the competing discount is larger.

Your content stays yours

What we produce for your brand is a lasting asset that works for you; its rights are settled in the collaboration agreement itself.

Attribution on your systems

We never ask you to trust a figure of ours: the code is recorded in your own affiliate system or the network you subscribe to. The number is yours.

Full confidentiality

The terms and figures of any collaboration never leave us, and we have no objection to an NDA before a detailed discussion.

Clear disclosure to the reader

We disclose the commercial relationship inside the content, as search engine policy requires — your content is never read as a hidden ad.

White-hat marketing only

No doorway pages, no keyword stuffing, no technique that breaches search engine guidelines. What we build in your name cannot expose you to a penalty.

And the measuring happens on your side

We never ask you to take a number on our word. The exclusive code makes every order recordable in your own affiliate system, or in the network you subscribe to — so the number you decide on is your own. The simplest start is a short pilot you measure yourselves.
