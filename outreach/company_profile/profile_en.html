Who we are
The platform

A Saudi platform built on verified offers

Deal Pulse KSA is a wholly Saudi-owned platform that brings brands' offers and buying content together in one place, and delivers them to the shopper at the exact moment they are searching. We run on one rule we do not bend: no offer is published before it is verified — because an expired code damages the brand's reputation before it damages ours.

Vision

The most trusted reference for a real deal

To be the first destination and the most trusted reference for discovering offers in the Kingdom and the region — not through the longest list, but through the most accurate and honest content.

Mission

A smarter purchase in less time

To help the Saudi shopper make an informed decision that saves their time and money — and to help the brand reach them at the moment of intent, not before it.

Three channels, one audience

Website & content

A permanent page for every brand, an Arabic buying-guide library, and seasonal campaign pages — on a modern, fast, well-indexed technical base.

Telegram bot

Direct reach to the shopper: search, instant offers, favourites and alerts — with no app download and no sign-up friction.

Mini app

A full browsing interface inside Telegram, wired to the same source and updated at the same moment.

What we offer a brand is one thing: ready purchase intent. Someone searching for an offer or a discount code has already chosen the product — all that remains is completing the order. That is the shortest path between content and a sale, and the least wasteful in spend.

Trust & commitments
Guaranteed in writing

Your brand in hands that will not trade on it

Working with a third party that publishes under your brand's name is a risk before it is an opportunity. These commitments are what make that risk a measured one — and they form part of the agreement, not a verbal promise.

No unverified offer published

Every offer is tested before it goes live and pulled the moment it expires. We do not leave a dead code carrying your name.

No bidding on your brand name

We do not bid on your brand name or its variants in paid search. We do not compete for your own visitor, and we do not inflate your keyword cost.

No competitor code beside yours

Your brand page carries no competing offer and no diversion to one — even where the competing discount is larger.

Your content stays yours

What we produce for your brand is a lasting asset that works for you, governed by whatever the agreement sets out.

Reporting without varnish

We present figures exactly as the system recorded them, with their sources and dates — including what did not work.

Full confidentiality

The terms and figures of the agreement never leave us. We are ready to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion.

Clear disclosure to the reader

We disclose the commercial relationship inside the content, as search engine policy requires — your content is never read as a hidden ad.

White-hat marketing only

No doorway pages, no keyword stuffing, no technique that breaches search engine guidelines. What we build in your name cannot expose you to a penalty.

And on detailed numbers

We measure by quality of intent rather than volume of visits: whoever reaches us is at the end of the buying journey, not the start. Detailed performance figures are shared under an NDA — or, more straightforwardly, we begin with a short pilot campaign and you measure the result yourselves, on your own systems.
